An app that slams shut every time you open it leaves you staring at the Home Screen with flour on your hands. Now, if Spoonjoy closes twice in a row before it has finished opening, the next launch shows a calm, plain page instead, with your most recent recipes on this device and a Try again button that reopens the full app.
