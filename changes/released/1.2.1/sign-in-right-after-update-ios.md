platforms: [ios]

Right after you installed or updated Spoonjoy, the first tap on Continue with Google or GitHub could close the sign-in before anything appeared. Spoonjoy now keeps trying for up to half a minute while the status reads Opening sign-in, with a Cancel button, so one tap is enough.
