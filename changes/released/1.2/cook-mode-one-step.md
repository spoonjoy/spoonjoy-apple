The old cook mode did not survive the autumn. On iPhone, every step now gets a page of its own. Swipe to turn it, watch a single timer ring sweep around, and tick off the step's ingredients right where your thumb already is. It grows out of the Cook button when you start and shrinks back into it when you're done.
