When Apple kept your email to itself and Spoonjoy had never met that Apple ID, Sign in with Apple ended in a shrug and a generic error. Now Spoonjoy tells you it couldn't find your account yet, and when you sign in with Google, GitHub or your password within half an hour, it connects your Apple ID to that account and says so. A mistyped password along the way doesn't undo it. Next time, Apple lets you straight into the kitchen.
