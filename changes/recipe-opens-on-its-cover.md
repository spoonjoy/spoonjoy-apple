A recipe without a photo used to open on a shrug that read "Photo not added". Now every recipe opens on its cover, and one with no photo yet wears its own initial, large, in brass. Under the title sits the chef who wrote it, photo and all, and their name opens their kitchen. An imported recipe says where it came from and takes you there, and the story behind the dish sits on a brass rule, like a note in the margin of a good cookbook.
