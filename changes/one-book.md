The app and the website now read like the same cookbook. Every recipe opens on its cover, or its initial in a deep, readable brass, with the chef's photo byline, where it came from, and its story on a brass rule. Your Kitchen opens with your photo and handle. Chef pages, step names, amounts ("¼ cup", "Serves 4"), icons and empty states match the website. Sign-in says your spoonjoy.app account works here. A cookbook without a photo is a paper cover with its title and recipe count. Need, Basket and All sit under the shopping list's add field. A long chef handle breaks between words. Every button, pill and panel has iOS's smooth corners. Checking an ingredient fills its box with brass and draws the tick.
