You made a new recipe on the train, added a step, and asked Siri for milk the moment you were back online. Siri and the app both reached for your unsent edits at once, and the step could come out stuck on "not found" for good, or a tick on a just-added item could vanish. Now Siri waits its turn, or leaves your edit for the app to send, and the step and the tick both arrive.
